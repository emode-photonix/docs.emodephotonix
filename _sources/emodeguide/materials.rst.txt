
Material Database
-----------------

Descriptions of all materials available in built-in material database. This information is also accessible from ``refractive_index`` and ``material_explorer``. Unless specified otherwise, all materials have :math:`\mu = 1.0`. Each equation for the refractive index assumes that the wavelength :math:`\lambda` has units of :math:`\mu\mathrm{m}`.

    .. math:: n = \left(1 + \frac{5.79211 \times 10^{-2}}{238.019 - \lambda^{-2}} + \frac{1.67917 \times 10^{-3}}{57.3620 - \lambda^{-2}}\right)
    
    Wavelength range: 200 nm to 2000 nm
    
        | P. E. Ciddor, "Refractive index of air: new equations for the visible and near infrared," Appl. Opt. 35, 1566 (1996).

.. data:: AlGaAs
    
    .. math::
        
        \mathrm{Al}_x &\mathrm{Ga}_{1-x}\mathrm{As} \\\ \\
        E_0 &= 3.65 + 0.871 x + 0.179 x^2 \\
        E_\mathrm{d} &= 36.1 - 2.45 x \\
        E_\Gamma &= 1.424 + 1.266 x + 0.26 x^2 \\
        E_\mathrm{f} &= (2 E_0^2 - E_\Gamma^2)^{0.5} \\
        \eta &= \pi E_\mathrm{d}/(2 E_0^3 (E_0^2 - E_\Gamma^2)) \\
        M_1 &= \frac{\eta}{2 \pi} (E_\mathrm{f}^4 - E_\Gamma^4) \\
        M_3 &= \frac{\eta}{\pi} (E_\mathrm{f}^2 - E_\Gamma^2) \\
        E &= h c/ \lambda ,\\
        &\mathrm{where}\:h\:\mathrm{is\:Planck's\:constant\:and}\:c\:\mathrm{is\:the\:speed\:of\:light\:in\:vacuum.} \\
        \chi &= M_1 + M_3 E^2 + \frac{\eta}{\pi} E^4 \mathrm{ln}((E_\mathrm{f}^2 - E^2)/(2 E_\Gamma - E^2)) \\
        n &= (\chi + 1)^{0.5} \\
        
    
    Wavelength range: 689 nm to 1033 nm
    
        | M. A. Afromowitz, "Refractive index of Ga_{1-x}Al_{x}As," Solid State Commun. 15, 59 (1974).
        | Notes: requires Al fraction input (x between 0 and 1), which defaults to x=1: "AlGaAs, x".

Non-optical and nonlinear properties
-------------------------------------

Every material carries these scalar properties, read back with ``get('<material>')`` and
used by ``heat()``, ``electrostatic()``, and the chi(2) EME. Uniaxial crystals store the
optic-axis (extraordinary) value of ``dn_dT`` and ``eps_r_dc``. ``d`` (chi(2)) and ``r``
(the linear electro-optic, or Pockels, tensor) are both contracted coefficients in the
crystal frame (pm/V); only the nonzero independent coefficients are listed, and ``--``
means all zero (or, for ``r``, not applicable -- a material with no ``r`` method). A
thermal conductivity of ``1e-10``, a ``dn_dT`` of ``0``, or a resistivity of ``inf`` is
the database default, used where no reliable published value exists. Every non-default
value has a citation in the material's ``property_refs`` dict
(``get('<material>')['property_refs']``); consult the source code in ``EMP_materials.py``
for the full citation text.

Not available (default values used): ``dn_dT`` for HfO2, TiO2, TiO2_rutile, GaP, GaSb,
InAs, InSb, InGaAs, InGaP, AlGaAs, and GGG; ``d`` for InP. ``r`` is currently only entered
for LN, LN_MgO, GaAs, InP, AlN, and GaN -- the materials the electrostatic phase-shifter
example uses; every other material's ``r`` is the base-class zero tensor.
